LinkedIn Privacy Policy

Effective date: 30 September 2026 · Last updated: 30 September 2026

This policy explains how TNT Marketing Pvt. Ltd. ("TrendsAndTreat", "we", "us") handles your data when you connect your LinkedIn account to TrendsAndTreat at trendsandtreat.in. It covers only the LinkedIn connection. Everything else on TrendsAndTreat is covered by our full Privacy Policy.

1. Why we connect to LinkedIn

TrendsAndTreat lets creators write and schedule posts once and publish them to several networks. When you connect LinkedIn, we can publish the posts you create or schedule in TrendsAndTreat to your LinkedIn profile. We do nothing else with your LinkedIn account.

2. Permissions we request

PermissionWhat it lets us do
openidConfirm which LinkedIn member is connecting.
profileRead your name and profile picture, so we can show which account is connected.
emailRead the primary email address on your LinkedIn account.
w_member_socialCreate posts, including images, on your LinkedIn profile when you publish or schedule them in TrendsAndTreat.

We do not request access to your connections, messages, job history, company pages, or advertising accounts.

3. Data we receive from LinkedIn and store

DataWhy we keep it
LinkedIn member IDRequired to publish a post as you.
Name and profile picture linkTo show you which LinkedIn account is connected.
Email addressTo show which account is connected and to contact you about a failed post.
Access token and, if provided, refresh tokenTo publish your posts. Stored encrypted; decrypted only in memory while a post is being published.
Links and IDs of posts we published for youSo you can open your published post from TrendsAndTreat.

We do not read your LinkedIn feed, other people's posts, or engagement statistics.

4. How we use LinkedIn data

  • Only to publish content you create or approve, at the time you choose.
  • We never post without your action or your schedule.
  • We do not sell LinkedIn data, share it with data brokers, or use it for advertising, profiling, or recruiting.
  • We do not combine LinkedIn data with data from other sources to build profiles of you.
  • We follow the LinkedIn API Terms of Use.

5. Who we share it with

  • LinkedIn: the post text and images you choose to publish.
  • Our hosting provider [NAME YOUR HOST]: stores the data above so the Service can run, under a confidentiality agreement.
  • Legal: only when required by law or a valid court order.

Other TrendsAndTreat users cannot see your LinkedIn email, tokens, or member ID.

6. Security

LinkedIn tokens are encrypted at rest, all connections use HTTPS, and only the systems that publish posts can decrypt them.

7. How long we keep it

  • We keep LinkedIn data only while your LinkedIn account is connected.
  • If you disconnect LinkedIn or ask us to remove it, we delete your LinkedIn tokens, member ID, name, email and profile picture link within 7 days.
  • If LinkedIn tells us your access has been revoked, or the token expires and you do not reconnect, we delete this data within 30 days.
  • If you delete your TrendsAndTreat account, all LinkedIn data is deleted with it.

Posts already published on LinkedIn stay there until you delete them on LinkedIn.

8. How to disconnect and delete your data

  1. On LinkedIn, open Settings → Data privacy → Permitted services and remove TrendsAndTreat. Our access stops immediately.
  2. Email contact@trendsandtreat.in with the subject "Delete my LinkedIn data" from the email on your TrendsAndTreat account. We delete it within 7 days and confirm by email.

9. Your rights

You can ask to see, correct, or delete the LinkedIn data we hold about you, or withdraw your consent at any time by emailing contact@trendsandtreat.in. We reply within 30 days. These rights apply under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, the EU/UK GDPR, and other laws where they apply to you.

10. Changes

If we change what we do with LinkedIn data, we will update this page and the date at the top, and ask for your consent again before using any new permission.
